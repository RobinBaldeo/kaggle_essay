Follow these instructions exactly and return the results in a JSON string.

### Instructions

#### What to Analyze
- **Current Report**: Wells Fargo Q4 2024 financial report (dated January 15, 2025, covering October-December 2024).
- **Previous Reports**: Compare against all prior quarterly reports:
  - 2023 Q1, 2023 Q2, 2023 Q3, 2023 Q4
  - 2024 Q1, 2024 Q2, 2024 Q3
- **Focus**: Look *only* at charts, images, and tables (ignore all text outside these, like section headings or paragraphs unless part of a chart/table title).
- **Goal**: Find at least 8 discrepancies where Q4 2024’s charts, images, or tables show data from a past quarter (e.g., 2023 Q2 loans) differently from how that quarter’s original report showed it. Examples:
  - Different titles (e.g., "Mortgage Loans" in 2023 Q2 vs. "Retail Mortgage Loans" in Q4 2024 for 2023 Q2 data).
  - Changed structure (e.g., Q4 2024 adds/removes rows or lines for 2023 Q2 data).
  - Wrong numbers (e.g., Q4 2024 shows 2023 Q2 loans as $400B when 2023 Q2 reported $392B).
  - Incorrect legend/key labels (e.g., misnamed data series for 2023 Q2 in Q4 2024).

#### Steps
1. **Open Q4 2024 Report**:
   - Find every chart, image, and table.
   - Note the page number, title, structure (e.g., rows, columns, lines), and any historical data shown (e.g., 2023 Q2 loans, 2024 Q1 charge-offs).

2. **Match to Previous Reports**:
   - For each chart, image, or table in Q4 2024 that shows past quarter data (e.g., 2023 Q2, 2024 Q1):
     - Locate the same topic (e.g., loans, charge-offs) in the original report for that quarter (e.g., 2023 Q2 report).
     - Focus only on charts, images, or tables in the original report.

3. **Find Discrepancies**:
   - Compare how Q4 2024 presents the past quarter’s data to the original report:
     - **Titles**: Does Q4 2024 use a different title for the same data?
     - **Structure**: Does Q4 2024 change the layout (e.g., adds a row, removes a line) for that past data?
     - **Numbers**: Does Q4 2024 show a different number for the past quarter than the original report?
     - **Legends/Keys**: Does Q4 2024 label the past data incorrectly in the chart?
   - Only count it as a discrepancy if Q4 2024’s chart, image, or table misrepresents or alters the past quarter’s data compared to the original report’s chart, image, or table.
   - Do NOT count:
     - Updates to current Q4 2024 values (e.g., Q4 2024 loans vs. Q3 2024 loans).
     - Natural changes over time unless Q4 2024 misreports a specific past quarter’s data.

4. **Build the Output**:

     - `description`: Why it’s wrong (e.g., "Q4 2024 overstates 2Q23 loans by $7.1B").
   - Return a JSON array with at least 8 discrepancies.

5. **If Reports Are Missing**:
